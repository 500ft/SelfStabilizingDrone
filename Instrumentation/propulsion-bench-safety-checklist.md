# Propulsion Bench Safety Checklist

This checklist gates every energized propeller/thrust-stand session. A checked
list documents preparation; it does not replace competent supervision or local
laboratory rules.

## Before installing a propeller

- [ ] Test ID, operator, observer, hardware revision, and abort authority recorded.
- [ ] Motor direction, ESC telemetry, RPM, current, command limits, and physical
      kill switch verified **props off**.
- [ ] Bench supply current limit set, or battery circuit fitted with an
      appropriately rated fuse and smoke stopper for first power-up.
- [ ] Stand, torque arm, load cell, fasteners, and ballast mechanically secured;
      expected thrust cannot tip, translate, or shed the rig.
- [ ] Guard/shield contains a failed prop or fragment without contacting it.
- [ ] Propeller inspected, correctly oriented, undamaged, and rated for the test.

## Battery and personnel controls

- [ ] LiPo inspected for swelling, damage, connector heating, and cell imbalance.
- [ ] Charge state/voltage and battery ID recorded; charging occurred in a
      fire-resistant location using the correct chemistry and current.
- [ ] Fire-safe battery container and the site-approved response method are
      immediately available; egress is clear.
- [ ] Eye protection worn; loose clothing, hair, cables, and tools secured.
- [ ] Exclusion zone marked and clear; nobody stands in the propeller plane.
- [ ] Kill switch is within reach of the dedicated observer, not behind the rig.
- [ ] No energized rig is left unattended.

## Test and shutdown

- [ ] Start at the lowest command and stop for abnormal vibration, sound,
      telemetry loss, smoke, odor, loose hardware, or unexpected current.
- [ ] Voltage, current, RPM, thrust, motor/ESC/battery temperature, ambient
      temperature, and exclusions are logged for every run.
- [ ] Cooldown and maximum consecutive-run limits are defined before testing.
- [ ] After shutdown, command is zero, RPM is zero, power is disconnected, and
      stored energy is safe before anyone approaches.
- [ ] Damage, temperature, data integrity, and any abort are recorded before the
      next run.
